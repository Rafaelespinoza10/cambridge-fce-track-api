This is Reading Part 5 - Multiple Choice.

Write one continuous `stimulus` text of roughly 550-700 words — an article, a personal account, a profile, or a similar long-form piece appropriate for B2 First Reading. It does not contain numbered gaps (unlike Use of English parts).

For each item:

- `prompt` must be a full standalone question about the text — its main idea, a specific detail, the writer's opinion or attitude, the purpose of a section, an implied meaning, or a similar comprehension point. Questions must follow the order the relevant information appears in the text.
- `options` must contain exactly 4 full-sentence or full-phrase options. Only one must be fully supported by the text; the other 3 must be plausible misreadings (a detail that's almost right, a plausible but unstated inference, or a distortion of what the text actually says) — never options a careful reader could rule out on general knowledge alone.
- `answerKey` must be `{ "kind": "single_choice", "acceptedOptionIds": [<the one correct option's id>] }`.
